# Landing Page Visual Review

## Development review — 27 August 2026

The desktop public page was reviewed at the hero and product-value sections. The hero now presents the Light Signal V identity, primary and secondary actions, three factual value cues, and an explicitly labelled example workspace rather than implied activity metrics. The product section clearly communicates the real-product-to-flyer-and-selling-package journey, uses the approved Navy-and-Electric-Blue system, and retains a deliberate sign-in action.

The browser’s Preview mode message is platform-provided and is not part of the ViraSquare page. No customer testimonial, customer logo, results claim, or invented analytics was introduced.

The lower-page review confirmed that the control section, final Electric Blue invitation, and three-column footer remain visually separated and readable. The footer presents service actions that use the existing sign-in route, while the About, support, and policy information is plainly marked as launch preparation rather than being represented as active pages.

An unauthenticated 390 px mobile rendering was also reviewed. The Light Signal V header, headline, body copy, full-width primary action, secondary action, and factual value cues fit in a clear single-column order without horizontal clipping. Desktop-only navigation is intentionally hidden at this width while Sign in remains visible.
