# Landing Practical Visual Refinement

## Honest answer

**Yes, the current visual examples can cause a small amount of confusion.** The watch makes the product-post result easier to understand, but because it is the only concrete product a visitor sees, someone may wrongly assume ViraSquare is mainly for watches, fashion products, or one fixed flyer style. The small example flyer also does not yet show the level of output quality that ViraSquare can provide.

The answer is not to remove the visuals. It is to replace the single-category illustration with a **stronger, more practical, clearly labelled sample** that demonstrates quality while also making the product scope clearer.

## Recommended product demonstration

Use **one polished, fictional social flyer** for a broadly understandable everyday product. I recommend an original, fictional **beauty/wellness product** or a **small food-business product** because they are familiar, visually rich, and immediately understandable. The visual should be a full, high-quality square post rather than a tiny mini-card.

The product demonstration should have a small, plain label:

> **Illustrative sample. Built from saved product details.**

It should not show a real business name, a real customer, a claimed result, or a copied product. It should not make a fixed promise that every output will look identical. The page copy beside it should make the scope obvious: **“For products you sell, the services you offer, and the expertise you want customers to trust.”**

### Why this is better

| Current risk | Improved approach | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A single watch can make the tool feel category-specific. | Use one polished practical product sample and a short scope line covering products, services, and expertise. | Visitors understand ViraSquare is broader than one business type. |
| The miniature flyer hides the quality of the output. | Show one larger, composed example flyer in the product-value section. | Visitors can immediately feel the standard of the intended output. |
| A realistic example could look like a customer success claim. | State that it is an illustrative sample and use fictional brand/product details. | The page stays truthful and avoids fake social proof. |
| Several full example products could become cluttered. | Use one hero sample, with small category chips: **Fashion · Beauty · Food · Gadgets · Services**. | The page signals range without becoming a gallery. |

## What the final invitation needs

**Yes, the final blue section should have a graphic.** It has the right closing message, but the wide right side is doing very little. A graphic there would balance the section and make the final moment feel like a product invitation, not just a block of text.

The right graphic should be a simple ViraSquare **content system composition**, not a stock photo or another product flyer. It should show three overlapping abstract cards:

| Card | Meaning | Appearance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Direction card** | A useful idea for today. | Small Navy card with a short line system and Electric Blue signal marker. |
| **Visual card** | A ready-to-post creative. | White card with a compact abstract product/post composition. |
| **Reply card** | Useful selling support. | Sky Blue card with a short message/reply structure. |

The Light Signal V geometry should appear quietly in the background, tying the ending to ViraSquare’s identity. On desktop, the composition sits on the right of the closing message. On mobile, it moves below the actions in a compact stacked form. It may float very slightly, but should never turn into a distracting animation.

## What I will not do

I will not use a real customer product, a stock model, a copied brand, fake output, fake review, made-up performance number, generic robot art, or a continuously moving banner. The product sample will be an **illustrative example**, and the final graphic will explain the ViraSquare system rather than invent a promise.

## Approval choice

If you approve, I will make the following contained refinement only:

1. Replace the watch-specific micro-demo with one larger, polished, fictional practical product flyer and clear scope/category cues.
2. Add the abstract three-card ViraSquare content-system graphic to the blue final invitation.

The existing landing-page structure, product-selling-package explanation, footer, sign-in path, logo identity, and workspace will remain unchanged.
