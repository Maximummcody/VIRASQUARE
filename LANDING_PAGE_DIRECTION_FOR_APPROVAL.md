# ViraSquare Landing Page Direction for Approval

## Plain recommendation

The current landing page has a strong **first section**: it looks clean, the headline is memorable, and the main action is clear. However, it asks a new visitor to make a decision before showing enough of the actual product. ViraSquare does much more than give a daily idea. It helps a business owner plan, create a truthful product flyer, prepare a caption and buyer reply, make useful educational content, and keep the work organised. The landing page should reveal that value in a short, confident story.

> **The goal is not to make the page longer for its own sake. The goal is to make the value obvious before the visitor reaches the final call to action.**

The recommended direction is a **light, editorial product story**: Deep Navy for important confidence moments, Electric Blue for action and energy, generous white space, real-looking ViraSquare interface previews, and a few bold visual breaks. It should feel professional and alive, not like a generic AI website or a crowded advertising page.

## Critical improvement to the current hero

The current preview uses a day streak, a weekly percentage, and a total-post count. Even when intended as an example, these can look like real performance analytics. ViraSquare should not accidentally imply tracking or results it does not have. In the redesign, that preview should instead say **Example workspace** and show three true capabilities: **Today’s direction**, **Your weekly rhythm**, and **Ready to create**. This keeps the visual strength without looking like invented analytics.

## Visual design direction

The page should not become a wall of matching rounded cards. The visual rhythm should alternate between open light sections, one or two Deep Navy confidence moments, an asymmetrical product-flyer composition, and a clean product-workflow strip. Electric Blue should guide the eye to the actions and important words, not cover every background.

For the public examples, I recommend **designed product mockups**, not claims about real customer results. The illustrations can show the ViraSquare interface and content-output formats, but should be marked as examples where needed. We should avoid fake reviews, fake sales figures, invented user logos, “trusted by” rows, or made-up testimonials. Those would reduce trust instead of adding it.

## Motion that feels polished rather than messy

Motion should support understanding, not show off. The page should respect reduced-motion preferences and remain fully clear when animations are off.

| Moment | Recommended movement | Why it helps | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hero preview | A very slow 6–8 px float and a gentle Electric Blue highlight sweep across one true workflow label. | Gives the opening visual life without looking like a gaming or crypto site. | No looping text changes or fake number counters. |
| Workflow steps | Each step fades and rises slightly when it first enters view. | Makes the visitor follow the story in the right order. | Once only; about 180–240 ms per step. |
| Product transformation | A restrained slide from “saved product” to “ready-to-use flyer and selling package.” | Explains the flagship value in seconds. | Trigger once while scrolling; never autoplay a long sequence. |
| Calls to action | Blue focus glow, small upward hover response, and responsive press feedback. | Makes buttons feel deliberate and trustworthy. | Under 180 ms; keyboard use remains instant. |

## Footer recommendation

The footer should be useful and calm, not another large sales section. Its main role is to let visitors find the right next step, understand that ViraSquare is a real service, and reach important policy information.

| Footer area | Items to include now | Destination or behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Start with ViraSquare** | Start your content rhythm; Make a product post; Sign in | All three use the existing sign-in path. They do not create content automatically. |
| **Explore** | How it works; Product posts; Educational content; Your weekly rhythm | Scroll to the relevant landing-page section. |
| **Company** | About ViraSquare; Contact and support | These need short, accurate pages and a real support contact before they are enabled. |
| **Legal and privacy** | Privacy Policy; Terms of Use; Request data deletion | These must be built from ViraSquare’s actual data, OAuth, storage, and AI behaviour before they are linked publicly. |
| **Identity** | Light Signal V, two-tone ViraSquare wordmark, current year, and a short one-line promise | Reinforces the brand without adding social icons for accounts that do not yet exist. |

Until the About, support, privacy, terms, and data-deletion pages are written accurately, the footer should not pretend those pages are available. We can either keep those links visually marked as “Coming with launch preparation” or build the trust pages as the next separate approved piece of work.

## What stays unchanged

This is a public-page expansion only. The current sign-in path, Today-first workspace, Calendar, products, product flyer generation, Selling Package, educational carousel flow, Library, archive rule, mobile navigation, and all customer-generated content remain unchanged. Product creation and any future paid activity stay deliberate; the page must not generate a flyer or create user data just because someone scrolls through it.

## Suggested build order after approval

First, redesign the landing content and its visual examples in a static, responsive build while keeping the hero message. Next, add the practical footer with active sign-in actions and scroll links. Finally, create the About, support, Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and data-deletion pages as a separate **Brand and Trust Foundation** step, using information that reflects the actual application rather than copied generic text.

## Approval question
