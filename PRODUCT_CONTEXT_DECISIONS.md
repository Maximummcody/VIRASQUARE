# Product Context Decisions

**Status:** Discussion agreed; implementation is not yet authorized.

## Guiding principle

Ask only for information that materially changes ViraSquare’s ideas, claims, content angles, proof, calls to action, or product-specific recommendations. Do not turn onboarding or product entry into a long generic questionnaire.

## First-time onboarding

Keep the existing first-time onboarding short. Do not add the extended business-context questions to it.

## Post-value business context

After a user has received their first useful ViraSquare value, invite them to an optional Business context experience. It should be described in plain language as helping ViraSquare make content more like their business.

The user may complete or exit the experience. It must not trap them in a form.

The agreed core questions are:

1. **What makes you different?** *(Why do customers choose you, return to you, or recommend you?)*
2. **What do customers usually ask or worry about before buying?** *(For example: price, fit, authenticity, how to use it, delivery, or whether it is right for them.)*
3. **What should a first-time customer understand before choosing from you?** *(What helps them choose well and avoid a wrong expectation?)*
4. **What are you focusing on right now?** *(A product, service, offer, season, goal, or type of customer.)*
5. **What must we never say or promise?** *(Claims, prices, availability, results, or wording the owner cannot stand behind.)*

Do **not** add a separate “Facts ViraSquare can safely say” question at this stage.

## Product invitation and reminders

After the Business context experience is completed or exited, show a gentle invitation to add a first product. This must feel like a welcoming next step, not a heavy second form.

If the business has no saved products, keep light, non-aggressive reminders available. Do not force product entry during general educational content.

## Product-led content rule

When a user explicitly selects product-led content, ViraSquare must require them to add or select a product before it generates that content. If they do not want to add a product, they can leave that path and explore other content directions instead.

## Product quick add

Keep quick product entry fast. The planned core is product name, category, price or “ask for price,” and a real product image.

Replace the vague “Verified details” wording with:

> **Help us understand this product**
> *Share facts you are sure of. ViraSquare will use them to make product content accurate and useful.*

The two extended product questions are optional but strongly recommended:

1. **Who is this for, or when would they use it?**
2. **What makes this worth choosing?**

Do not add a bulk-product feature. The optional questions must not slow owners who want to add many products one by one.

## Card branding direction

Logo and Instagram username are optional but strongly recommended.

- **Card header:** small logo and brand name, with a clean text fallback where no logo is provided.
- **Card footer:** a clear Instagram indicator and handle, for example an Instagram icon followed by `@username`. If no handle is supplied, the footer remains clean.
- **Closing cards:** support an optional short brand sign-off. Explain this as a line ViraSquare may use only on closing cards to make the content feel recognisably theirs.

## Guardrail

Before implementation, explain and request approval for any change that would materially alter an existing workflow, including onboarding, Calendar, Today, product selection, or visual generation.
