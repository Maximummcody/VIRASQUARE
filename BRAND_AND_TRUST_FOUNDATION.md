# ViraSquare Brand and Trust Foundation

## Proposed brand promise

**ViraSquare helps small businesses turn real products and real customer needs into content they can confidently use.**

## Short description

ViraSquare is a thoughtful daily content partner for small businesses. It helps owners prepare product flyers, captions, buyer replies, educational posts, and a realistic weekly content rhythm from their own saved business and product details.

## Design boundaries

The final ViraSquare identity should use an original vector-first mark, a navy and electric-blue core palette, and simple reproducible geometry. It must avoid generative-art texture, gradients used as decoration, generic AI-symbol styling, stock motifs, and ambiguous imagery. The logo should remain clear in a browser tab, mobile app icon, social profile, and Meta settings.

## Trust assets before Group 4

Before public Instagram/Facebook publishing, ViraSquare should have a public Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, data-deletion request page, support contact, final app name and logo, and a custom domain. The final Group 4 Meta submission should also include the selected use case, a secure redirect URL, a data-deletion request URL, and a clear recording of the owner connection and publishing journey.

## Verified Meta readiness references

Meta's current Instagram app setup documentation says that a business verified connection is required before publishing an app, and its business-login setup calls for redirect, deauthorization, and data-deletion request URLs. Meta's Instagram Platform overview says that serving Instagram professional accounts that the app owner does not manage requires Advanced Access, App Review, and Business Verification. The content publishing documentation also requires publishing media to be publicly accessible at the time Meta fetches it.

## Important legal note

The chosen logo concept should undergo a professional trademark and availability review in the markets ViraSquare will serve before public launch. This document is not legal advice.

## Trademark similarity-review note

The user-shared glossy V-and-square board is a direction reference only, not a final asset. Before selecting a final ViraSquare mark, review the proposed wordmark and the prominent visual elements separately, including the V shape, square/container relationship, any detached square element, and the product/service categories. The USPTO explains that a useful clearance search looks for marks that may be identical or similar in wording or design and may create customer confusion. It identifies design elements through six-digit design search codes and says similarity can include visual appearance, sound, meaning, or overall commercial impression. This internal review should reduce obvious risk only; a qualified trademark professional should review any final filing or launch-critical decision.

## Core Mark refinement review

Two approval-only, original flat-vector refinements were prepared on 27 August 2026. **Signal Square** uses a navy rounded-square field, a high-contrast V, and one detached electric-blue square. It is the clearer icon candidate and remains legible as a standalone mark. **Integrated Square** uses an electric-blue rounded-square container with a V and integrated square detail. It expresses the V-and-square concept more literally but has more small elements and needs further simplification before favicon use. Both concepts are intentionally flat and avoid the glossy rendering, decorative gradients, or AI-art treatment in the user-shared reference board.

An additional approval-only **light Signal V** refinement uses Electric Blue as the leading icon field, White as the open surface, and Navy only for structure. This responds to the concern that the first Signal Square version made the navy field feel too dominant. It is a comparison treatment only; no live logo, app icon, or favicon has changed.
