# ViraSquare Brand Identity

## Approved direction

ViraSquare uses the **Light Signal V** as its official mark. It is an original, flat, vector-first symbol: a Navy and Electric Blue geometric V inside an open rounded square, with a small signal block and a quiet base line. The mark is designed to stay recognisable without relying on gloss, 3D effects, gradients, or generic AI imagery.

The primary wordmark is **ViraSquare**, with **Vira** in Deep Navy (`#0B1220`) and **Square** in Electric Blue (`#2563EB`). This colour split is intentionally limited to the product identity; it is not applied to customer brands or generated flyers.

## Usage rules

Keep clear space equal to at least **one quarter of the mark width** on every side. Do not stretch, rotate, recolour individual V halves outside the defined palette, remove the signal block or base line, add shadows or gradients, or place the light mark over a busy image.

Use the mark at **32 px or larger** in interfaces. Use the horizontal lockup at **140 px or wider**. At smaller browser-tab and app-icon sizes, use the icon only; the wordmark would become too small to read clearly.

## Live implementation boundary

The approved identity is now used in the public landing page, onboarding/loading surfaces, authenticated workspace header, favicon, Apple touch icon, and PWA manifest. Existing navigation, content generation, saved business brands, customer logos, user-uploaded products, flyers, visual cards, and all other workflow behaviour remain unchanged.
