# ViraSquare Project Summary

**Status:** Current as of 27 August 2026

**Source repository:** [Maximummcody/VIRASQUARE](https://github.com/Maximummcody/VIRASQUARE)  
**Latest recorded checkpoint:** `0c070cdd`

> ViraSquare is a focused daily content partner for small businesses. It turns saved business details, real products, and owner-confirmed customer context into useful content that an owner can review and use with confidence.

## What is complete

### 1. Core business and content foundation

ViraSquare has a short onboarding journey and a progressive Business context area, so owners can add more useful business information after receiving initial value rather than being overwhelmed at registration. It supports saved brand identity details, optional logo, Instagram handle, customer concerns, customer-facing context, and a closing signature. Educational content remains separate from product-led content unless an owner deliberately chooses a product route.

| Capability | Current outcome |
|---|---|
| Business context | Captures only high-value facts that can improve future content without forcing a long onboarding form. |
| Product catalogue | Stores individual products, images, prices, details, and category-aware facts. Extended product fields remain optional but clearly valuable. |
| Content planning | Gives owners a Today-first Daily Brief, weekly rhythm, contextual Calendar, intentional Rest days, and an alternative-content path. |
| Content library | Keeps Drafts, Ready to post, Posted, and Archived work organised with search, filters, sort, pagination, help, and friendly empty states. |

### 2. Product-post value: Group 1 and Group 2

The strongest current ViraSquare flow is the deliberate product route. An owner selects a real saved product and receives a post-ready flyer, matching caption, buyer reply help, and a different next selling angle.

| Capability | Current outcome |
|---|---|
| Product flyer generation | Uses **GPT Image 2** securely on the server. The default path protects the real uploaded product as much as possible; Stylish generation is optional and clearly explains that presentation details may change. |
| Flyer quality controls | Uses varied flyer directions, owner correction-aware regeneration, stored review records, and downloads that save as files rather than opening over the workspace. |
| Selling package | Supplies a truthful caption, a concise buyer/DM reply, and one next product angle based on saved facts. |
| Product education | Remains an optional, separate follow-up. It does not replace the original flyer, product post, or Calendar entry. |
| Product discovery | Shows a no-cost capability preview, a gentle first-product invitation, and a deliberate Make product post starter. No paid flyer is generated automatically. |

### 3. Trustworthy product archive lifecycle

Products are no longer removed immediately. The owner can archive a product, see its exact days remaining, restore it within 30 days, or deliberately use the protected Empty Trash action. A daily secure cleanup removes expired archived products after the recovery period.

Existing flyers, captions, selling packages, downloads, posted records, planned content, and Library history remain available. Archived products cannot be used for new product-led work; planned work instead asks the owner to restore or choose an active replacement product.

### 4. Group 3: business memory and optional learning

ViraSquare now uses saved business context, products, brand information, recent plan context, and only the owner’s own confirmed outcomes to improve later suggestions. The optional **What worked?** moment remains small and non-intrusive. It never presents owner feedback as platform analytics, promises a repeat result, changes a plan automatically, or overwrites old content.

### 5. Experience, mobile, and reliability work

The main workspace is responsive on desktop and mobile. The top navigation remains on desktop, while phone users have a fixed bottom navigation. Mobile pull-to-refresh is available only where it is safe: Today, Calendar, My Products, and Library. It is intentionally excluded from typing, correction, review, upload, and generation screens.

The live interface now uses the approved **Deep Navy, Electric Blue, White, and Soft Gray** visual system. The change was deliberately theme-only: no established workflow, information architecture, product action, or user-generated flyer/card colour was replaced.

| Reliability and mobile improvement | Current outcome |
|---|---|
| Faster entry | The signed-in workspace and private review code load only when needed. Unreachable legacy entry code was removed. |
| Smoother returns | Recent data is kept briefly to reduce needless reloads, while manual refresh, explicit changes, reconnection, and recovery remain immediate. |
| Mobile home-screen support | ViraSquare has a proper name, Navy browser chrome, standalone launch metadata, and a branded app icon when saved to a phone. |
| Current verification | Automated coverage, TypeScript checks, production builds, desktop/mobile review, and real-owner acceptance checks have been completed across the recent work. |

## Current brand direction

The approved product-interface palette is **Deep Navy + Electric Blue + White + Soft Gray**. Generated customer flyers and educational cards retain each business’s own visual identity; they are not forced into the ViraSquare interface palette.

The current V icon is functional for app-icon and favicon use. The final identity is **not yet selected**. Three original vector-first concept directions have been prepared for selection:

| Direction | Character | Suggested fit |
|---|---|---|
| Signal V | A clear V inside a square signal frame. | Strongest all-round direction, especially for a small app icon or favicon. |
| Open Square | A confident V that opens a square. | More editorial and expressive. |
| Rhythm V | Three lines form a V and echo a posting rhythm. | Most connected to the daily-content idea, but slightly more detailed at very small sizes. |

The proposed brand promise is:

> **Turn what you sell into content you can confidently use.**

## Brand and trust work before Group 4

The next non-product step is a **Brand and Trust Foundation**. It does not change ViraSquare’s core flow; it makes the product look and operate like a trustworthy public service.

| Item | What is needed |
|---|---|
| Final logo suite | Selected wordmark, V mark, light/dark variants, social-profile image, and updated icon/favicons. |
| Public copy | Final one-line promise, short description, professional support email, and contact details. |
| Trust pages | Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and a data-deletion request page. |
| Public identity | A custom domain and branded support email are strongly recommended before broad launch. |
| Meta readiness | A test Instagram Professional account, a connected Facebook Page if Facebook Login is used, secure redirect URLs, and review demonstration material. |

For public third-party Instagram publishing, Meta requires the app to have the appropriate access and review posture. Its current documentation says that publishing an app requires a verified business connection; apps serving Instagram accounts the app owner does not manage require Advanced Access, App Review, and Business Verification. Meta’s setup also calls for redirect, deauthorization, and data-deletion request URLs.[1] [2]

## Principles that remain protected

ViraSquare will continue to follow these decisions:

- Product facts, prices, availability, fit, proof, and claims remain truthful and owner-provided.
- Default product generation protects the real uploaded product; Stylish generation is optional, restrained, and clearly disclosed.
- ViraSquare does not create fake reviews, fake analytics, fake sales claims, or invented social-platform performance.
- The owner must take a clear action before content is published, scheduled, archived permanently, or materially changed.
- New features must not silently replace the established Today-first workflow, product value, Calendar flow, Library history, or customer content.
- Any material workflow, cost, external account connection, or data-storage change requires plain-language explanation and owner approval first.
