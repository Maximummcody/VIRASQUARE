# Mobile Readiness Audit

## Scope

This assessment covers the completed Group 1 and Group 2 mobile experience before Group 3. It includes the Today screen, mobile navigation, product flyer review, selling package, product-linked educational carousel, Library controls, product forms, dialogs, and touch interactions.

## Findings

The existing mobile layout has a sound base. The Today view remains readable at a 375 px viewport, content falls to a single column where appropriate, and the primary product and education dialogs already use scrollable mobile sheets.

The approved refinement introduces a mobile-only fixed bottom navigation while retaining the desktop header navigation. It increases the practical reachability of the five primary sections without changing destinations or workflows. The main page reserves bottom space so content does not sit behind the navigation.

The product flyer flow now presents key mobile actions at full width, enlarges compact refresh and download touch targets, and uses a safe-area-aware educational-carousel sheet. The order of flyer review remains flyer, correction, selling package, educational follow-up, and feedback.

## Deferred decisions

Colour and broader brand-identity changes remain intentionally deferred for owner discussion. The mobile refinement does not alter the product facts, generation rules, Library lifecycle, Calendar workflow, or Group 3 scope.

## Owner acceptance check

Use a real phone to open a product flyer, enter a correction, prepare or review a selling package, open and close the educational-carousel sheet, save a draft, and use the Library. Confirm that the bottom navigation, scroll behavior, keyboard interaction, and action buttons feel comfortable.
