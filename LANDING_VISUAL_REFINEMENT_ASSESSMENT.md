# Landing Visual Refinement Assessment

## User-supplied screenshot review — 27 August 2026

The first two ordered crops of the unusually wide content-flow attachment show the headline handoff from the problem statement into the three-card flow. The text and divider are clear; the progress indicators themselves appear farther below the crop, so their exact shared baseline must be reviewed in the third crop and in the component implementation.

The owner’s concern is valid: every progress indicator must use the same stable visual anchor at the bottom of its card. They should be treated as part of a shared three-step system, not as individual decorative lines that can appear irregular.

## Recommendation

I agree with the owner’s five observations. The landing page does not need more sections; it needs **stronger signals inside the sections it already has**. The changes below are intentionally contained, so the page becomes more understandable and more action-driving without becoming loud or crowded.

| Area | Owner concern | Recommended refinement | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Hero workspace** | It can feel like a standalone mock screen that appeared without a clear purpose. | Turn it into an active **“Today in ViraSquare”** moment: keep the daily direction, then add one small product-post tile using the same Nura Oil example and a visible **Make this post** action cue. It should visually answer, “What can I do here?” | Do not add another large panel or a fake user result. Keep the existing hero size and preserve the real Sign in route. |
| **Primary action band** | The narrow horizontal statement feels too small and passive for its job. | Make it a fuller, soft-blue **decision bridge** immediately after the hero. Use a short supporting sentence plus a dark, compact **See how ViraSquare works** action that scrolls to the existing workflow section. | Do not create a competing second hero. This is one clear next action, not another sign-up barrier. |
| **Three-step flow** | Progress lines look uneven because their filled amounts differ. | Replace variable “progress” amounts with three equally proportioned, perfectly aligned **step rails**. Use a small round active marker and a consistent Electric Blue completion line. | These are a process illustration, not completion statistics or performance data. |
| **Three content-format cards** | The education and trust cards are too text-led, so visitors may not quickly understand the output. | Use the same Nura Oil sample across all three cards: a product flyer, a three-card educational oil-care carousel, and a trust post explaining what a buyer should know. Add a small shared label: **One saved product, three useful content formats.** | Keep **Illustrative format** labels and no customer name, customer photo, customer claim, or performance promise. One sample across all three clarifies the output family rather than narrowing the product scope. |
| **Spacing after the hero bridge** | If the hero workspace changes, the following band and workflow needs to feel rebalanced. | Reduce the visual gap, give the bridge more vertical substance, and align its text and action with the 1120 px content grid used by the sections below. | Do not change the section order, navigation, footer, or workspace. |

## Why the same illustrative oil is the right choice

Using one fictional practical product across Product Post, Educational Content, and Trust Building will make the offer **easier to understand**, not more confusing. It demonstrates the real point: the same saved product can be used in different ways depending on the owner’s goal. The category cues in the product section will remain so the beauty example is clearly a visual example, not a category limit.

The sample will remain fictional and labelled. It will never be presented as a real customer result or as a promise that every generated output is identical.

## Expected page story after refinement

> **See today’s direction → see the action it can trigger → understand the three-step flow → see one product used in three formats → feel the product-and-selling support → decide to start.**

This makes the page more practical, more energetic, and more immediately understandable while protecting the calm, professional ViraSquare direction.
