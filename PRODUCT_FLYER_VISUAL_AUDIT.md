# Product Flyer Visual Audit

## Controlled preview reviewed

Two representative 1080×1350 flyers were rendered from the same real uploaded red G-Shock image using the approved Kora Time test brand.

| Mode | Composition selected | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Default | Detail-led flyer | Pass. The image, product name, one verified supporting fact, price, brand lockup, Instagram, and order action have a clear hierarchy. The real photo is presented without an AI-created surrounding scene. |
| Stylish | Campaign flyer | Pass. The darker campaign treatment is visibly more expressive, while the exact product facts and brand information remain controlled by the flyer renderer. |

## Findings

- The fixed pale-background / centred-image / dark-copy template has been removed from the reviewed product-post path.
- The Default and Stylish modes remain visibly distinct without changing the product-fact layer.
- Portrait source photos retain neutral side space because the renderer preserves the real product photo instead of cropping or stretching it. Generated 4:5 product visuals are expected to occupy more of the image frame.
- Educational rich-card families were not reviewed or altered by this correction.
