# ViraSquare Deferred Work Register

This document keeps ideas the owner wants to preserve for later discussion. An item here is **not permission to build**. Before implementation, ViraSquare must bring the selected items back in plain language for final review and obtain explicit approval.

## Current Direction to Protect

ViraSquare should not compete with general chat tools by offering generic captions or ideas alone. It should become a content partner that remembers a real business, understands its real products, prepares the next useful content, and improves from owner-confirmed feedback.

## Important Guardrails

- Do not add a feature merely because it is technically possible. Every material workflow change needs clear explanation and owner approval first.
- Preserve the short first-time onboarding and progressive business/product context journey.
- Do not add bulk product upload, forced social posting, fake analytics, automatic replacement of posted content, or generic AI scene generation without a separate approved decision.
- Product facts, prices, availability, claims, results, ingredients, fit, and proof must remain truthful and owner-provided.
- Educational content remains product-free unless the owner intentionally chooses a product-led route.
- All product-visual decisions must visibly reward the effort of adding a product.
